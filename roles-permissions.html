Case Roles Per-case team roles — activity-based permissions
Unlike system roles, case role permissions are defined per case activity rather than as a flat action matrix — for example, the ability to View/Edit the General Screen, or Add/Edit a user's own Case Activities. Every case must have at least one Lead Member and one Admin Member.
